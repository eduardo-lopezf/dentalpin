# Settlements (liquidations)

Settling with associate dentists who are paid a percentage.

The module is **optional**: if nobody in your clinic works on a percentage,
do not install it and the tab does not appear.

## The idea, in one sentence

An associate on a percentage is paid on one of two figures, and they are
**not the same figure**: what they **earned** (the work they did) and what
has been **collected** for that work. On a large case those are months
apart. Paying on earned means handing out money the clinic has not received;
paying on collected means the associate shares the collection risk.

The screen shows **both, always**, and marks which one carries the
percentage.
