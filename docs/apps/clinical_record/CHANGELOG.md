# Changelog — Expediente clínico (`clinical_record`)

The App's own version, which moves independently of its modules' (ADR 0036). Each module keeps its own `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 0.1

- Declared in the catalog on 2026-10-03 with its current modules. Not yet decoupled from the Apps it requires.
- `consents` joined on 2026-10-03: consent letters, informed and data-use (ADR 0045).
- 2026-10-04: each professional sets up their own letterhead (*Mi membrete*); only theirs.
- 2026-10-04: letterheads for the record, the consent letters and the health questionnaire — the clinic's own and one per professional, each document carrying its own professional's and never another's — set up from the App's settings page (ADR 0046).
- 2026-10-04: the App has its own settings page — the clinic's record format (sections, order, points reviewed) — linked from its card in Settings → Apps.
- 2026-10-03: the record asks for the reason for the visit (ten requirements); `consents` gains the *conformity* letter.
- 2026-10-03: the record can be printed or handed over, and each time leaves a disclosure in it — recipient, purpose, evidence and the document as it left (ADR 0033).
- 2026-10-03: the *Expediente* tab says what the record holds of what a dental record should (nine requirements); family history and prognosis exist to be recorded.
- 2026-10-03: the patient record has an *Expediente* tab, and every clinical module contributes its section to the composed record.
- 2026-10-03: a consent can be printed, signed on paper and filed as a scan, besides signing on screen.
